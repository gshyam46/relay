## Completed

A metadata-first page preflight bounds historical lead bytes before full rows load; list fit/freshness are compact.

## Known Issues

- Exact owner aliases abstain on unsupported paraphrases and languages; customer criteria coverage and operator review burden remain unmeasured.
- Ranking is within the returned page, not global top-K beyond 100 active records. PostgreSQL query cost, lock duration and production-scale behavior require measurement.
- Manual POST /api/leads still requires name and a contact channel. Existing enquiry evaluation can separate missing contact from fit, but contact-free manual intake is not implemented.
- The 90-day freshness policy remains provisional. General semantic qualification and held-out multilingual/adversarial factuality remain L3-02 work.
- The default-concurrency TLS fixture timeout remains a CI reliability concern; the complete suite is verified at concurrency 2 without changing its assertions or timeout.
- Existing Vite bundle-size warning, mobile/accessibility acceptance, portable browser CI and full inbox/composer/outcome journeys remain open.
- No deployment, real provider messaging or production/customer database operation occurred. SQLite evidence and skipped PostgreSQL tests do not certify Supabase/production readiness.

# L1-05 integrated execution and recovery verification

Product: **AI Lead Intelligence & Outbound Automation**.
Date: **2026-09-11**.
Status: local implementation and automated verification complete for this bounded slice; **L1 remains in progress**. No deployment, customer database migration or real provider send was performed.

## Completed and scope

The [contract](../L1-05_EXECUTION_RECOVERY.md) was recorded before dependent implementation. Work follows the existing modular-monolith/PostgreSQL decision and shared workspace transaction gate.

- Migration 0005 adds durable retry timing, attempt limits, active execution/fence, lease, immutable revision/key binding and append-only recovery evidence. Ambiguous old attempts retain their history and gain conservative holds.
- Authorization checks contact restrictions, exact approval, due times, holds, frozen attempt/deadline budget and provider-key expiry before committing a new fenced attempt. Network calls run after commit.
- Known nonacceptance may retry with persisted backoff/jitter and Retry-After. Defaults are at most three attempts across review revisions and one hour from first dispatch. A stricter configured action limit remains stricter.
- Provider calls have a 15-second timeout; dispatch ownership has a 60-second lease. Transport/server uncertainty or an expired owner cannot trigger blind resend. Accepted requests wait for delivery evidence.
- Exact callbacks atomically commit receipt, attempt/action outcome, audit and domain event. Stale/foreign/closed/terminal evidence cannot regress current work. Conversation effects use the original execution and reviewed envelope, including reconstruction when uncertainty preceded initial message storage.
- Owner recovery records evidence for verified acceptance or closure without retry. It requires the displayed execution/fence and the session actor. Neither decision renews budgets or asserts delivery. Resolved history cannot crowd unresolved work out of the active recovery list.
- Outbound and lead detail expose send outcome, timing, attempt history and recovery. Bulk execution reports actual new attempts separately from deferred/held requests.
- Shutdown stops admission, drains HTTP/background/worker/executor operations and closes the DB last. After 30 seconds it fails the process with durable ownership preserved for reconciliation.

This advances L1-06 only for action due-time enforcement and L1-07 only for exact correlation, atomic core callback state and ordinary message reconstruction. It does not implement general sequence/event scheduling, fairness, unmatched-event repair or automatic replay of failed ancillary callback effects.

HTTP additions: GET /api/actions/:id/recovery, GET /api/outbound/recovery, POST /api/actions/:id/recovery/resolve. These are owner-scoped. Resolution accepts expected_execution_id, expected_fence, decision, evidence_note and optional provider_reference; acceptance requires the reference. Reviewer/tenant authority comes from the session.

An actual new claim reports execution_result.dispatched=true even if transport later fails or becomes uncertain. Bulk executed counts new claims; deferred and held count requests with no new claim. These counts do not mean delivered.

Adapters receive approvedDispatch plus server-created execution_id/provider_intent_key. Live SendGrid terminal events require exact execution and revision metadata. Current event IDs are bounded at 2,048 characters, with long policy keys hashed. No provider-reference-only or latest-attempt fallback is introduced. Test-only action callbacks permit a single existing unambiguous attempt for fixture compatibility.

## Known issues and external gates

- Real PostgreSQL integration, multi-process dispatch/callback ordering, least-privilege deployment and production-shaped upgrade/restore are pending. Docker's executable exists but no local engine pipe or PostgreSQL service/binaries were available; no explicit disposable PostgreSQL target was configured. No application DB was used as a substitute.
- Live provider acceptance/idempotency, authenticated delivery/reply events and received-copy verification remain pending.
- Callback core transactions are atomic, but a failure after commit in ancillary message/follow-up work is only audited. Duplicate receipts do not yet resume that unfinished work. This remains the next L1-07 implementation.
- Legacy records without a safe exact attempt identity stay held. Recovery does not invent a binding, clear uncertainty through approval, or release NOT_SENT for resend.
- Full event/sequence scheduler, tenant fairness, failed-event retry, quotas/kill switch, shared manual/reply composer and the verified operator journey remain open.
- Browser, accessibility/mobile and human QA are not completed. The landing page remains planned in L4-07/L5-07; it was not implemented in this slice.
- Larger client bundle requires later route splitting/performance work. No launch readiness claim follows from local test counts.

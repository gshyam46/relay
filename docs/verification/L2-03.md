## Completed

An owner can now resolve reviewed duplicate imports by attaching a source to the same exact-contact enquiry or creating a separate repeated/shared/distinct enquiry. The comparison shows source facts, current candidates, restrictions and explicit decisions. The lead Enquiry view retains original and attached sources with exact row links.

Identity decisions preserve original raw cells, correction history, frozen selection and HELD outcomes. Existing enquiry facts and prepared reviews stay unchanged by source linking. Separate creation atomically persists one lead, initial context when known, LeadCreated event, resolution and audit. Exact retries return the saved decision; changed intent and stale source/candidate/restriction state reject.

Shared-contact replies now stop existing directly matched automation atomically while retaining an unassigned receipt for review. A durable marker prevents replay against newer intentional work, and delayed callbacks cannot manufacture no-response tasks after recorded ambiguity. Contact restrictions and confirmed delivery remain authoritative.

The [contract](../L2-03_IDENTITY_RESOLUTION.md), ADR-015 and task/ownership boundary were recorded before implementation. Scoped parallel owners delivered backend, safety and UI; root integrated shared API, migration registry, logs, HTTP tests and specifications.

## Contracts changed

Authenticated owner GET /api/imports/:id/rows/:rowid/identity-review and POST /api/imports/:id/rows/:rowid/identity-resolution; authenticated tenant GET /api/leads/:id/import-sources. Session workspace and actor are authoritative and the current owner is rechecked under the database gate.

Import detail adds row identity_resolution/can_resolve_identity, resolution history and separate linked/created/resolved-held/unresolved counts. Original progress keeps its original meaning. One immutable row decision associates exact source with its resulting enquiry; source-only links do not overwrite current facts. Scoped provenance checks exact field/value/assertion/time.

Candidate display is at most 50 existing enquiries and 20 source rows, with totals. Review scans at most 5000 existing matches and 10000 distinct relevant restriction/pending receipt records. Excess returns an unavailable review without a usable token. Public resolution summaries omit stored snapshots; source history materializes at most 100 rows within an 8 MiB serialized source budget, with has_more. The stored decision snapshot is capped at 512 KiB.

Reply safety preflights 100 matched leads/workflows/queued actions/automatic tasks per category. Overflow leaves mandatory policy pending. A callback ambiguity scan over 100 applicable-time receipts defers if it cannot establish safety. These are explicit safety limits, not measured production capacity.

Independent review reproduced a late duplicate gap after the first full suite had passed: normal commit excluded every lead carrying its import_batch_id, including separately resolved enquiries. A blank source name can become the company fallback on a newly created enquiry and produce a new name/company candidate for another row. The fix excludes only leads proved to originate from ordinary COMMITTED row outcomes in the same frozen batch. The new regression leaves the later row HELD, with two total leads and one new event. The final 858-test suite above includes this fix.

Review also tightened raw-versus-normalized legacy contact matching, bounded full policy/candidate state, removed repeated public snapshots, and limited source-history response materialization. Current source truth, raw cells, exact money and historical outcomes remain intact. The older 0010 PostgreSQL runtime-only fixture now applies current migrations before current ImportsService; its frozen upgrade/constraint assertions remain unchanged.

Subsequent visual review shortened repeated CSV identifiers into source labels while preserving exact links and manual source text. The final UI passed all 41 identity/import/context checks. After the final 8 MiB source budget and same-import exclusion fixes, identity was rerun against the final integrated backend and passed 11/11; the owned browser and server were closed. ## Known issues

- Real PostgreSQL independent connections, upgrade/restore and deployed roles remain unverified here.
- Identity review has deliberate scan/display/history bounds. Very large matching or policy sets require operational review; no production capacity claim follows from the local tests.
- Full existing-contact correction, archive/export and complete data-work acceptance remain L2-04. There is no destructive merge, person/alias graph or automatic fact promotion.
- Linked source facts remain separate until an explicit enquiry correction. That correction creates a new revision and invalidates affected old review/analysis; attachment alone does not.
- Ambiguous replies remain unassigned in Event recovery. Full provider-thread correlation, owner assignment and composer work remain L4-03. An oversized mandatory stop cannot be waived by closing the receipt.
- Source authenticity, business-fit intelligence, first customer/channel validation and useful pilot outcomes remain open.
- The large app bundle, full history pagination, mobile/accessibility and operational load/cost evidence remain later gates.
- The interactive public landing remains L4-07/L5-07; publication remains L6-04.

## Human QA

1. Import representative same-enquiry, repeated-enquiry and genuinely shared-contact rows. Explain the selected decision and verify distinct histories.
2. Attach a source with different interest/budget to the same exact-contact enquiry. Find both source versions and confirm current facts and reviewed outgoing content were not silently replaced.
3. Resolve an initial duplicate group and a late HELD row; reload or retry and reconcile original import progress with later resolution counts.
4. Verify opt-out across all directly matching contacts, and recognize why conflicting or missing contact pairs cannot link.
5. Receive an ambiguous ordinary reply and inspect stopped automation and the unassigned receipt. Verify retry cannot restart old work or stop later intentional work, and follow the documented overflow escalation.
6. Complete keyboard, phone, zoom and screen-reader checks; separately validate actual PostgreSQL and selected-provider threading before any pilot.
